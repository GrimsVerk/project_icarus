# Privacy policy

<!-- PRIV-2 -->
[App name] plans time in the sun from a UV forecast. It has no accounts and no analytics, and it keeps what you tell it on your phone.

<!-- PRIV-3 -->
What stays on your phone. Your skin type, settings, sessions and skin reports are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Export writes them to a file only when you ask, and you choose where the file goes. Deleting the history in Settings, or deleting the app, removes them.

<!-- PRIV-4 -->
Location. If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location (to about a kilometre) while you use it. It sends that location to Apple's WeatherKit to fetch the UV forecast for where you are, and nowhere else. If you type a place instead, the name you type goes to Apple to find it on the map. Apple's privacy policy covers both: apple.com/legal/privacy.

<!-- PRIV-5 -->
Weather. Forecasts come from Apple Weather through WeatherKit. The request carries the location and nothing that identifies you.

<!-- PRIV-6 -->
Notifications. The skin check, the lasting-colour question and the timer alerts are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server for them.

<!-- PRIV-7 -->
Ads. The app shows one small banner from Google AdMob on two screens. Every ad request is non-personalised: ads are not chosen from a profile of you, and the app never asks for your advertising identifier. To serve the ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google's software receives your IP address (and the rough location it implies), information about your device and the app, how you interact with the ad, and diagnostic data. Google's description of what it collects: developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

<!-- PRIV-8 -->
Consent. Where Google's rules require it, such as in the EEA and the UK, Google's own consent form appears once before the first ad. You can change your answer at any time under Settings › Privacy options for the ad banner. Refusing still shows a banner, with limited ads.

<!-- PRIV-9 -->
Children. The app is not made for children.
